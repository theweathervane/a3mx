## The camera limits

Every stage has the same limits: the fighters' layer scrolls with the screen's left edge from **256**
to **640**, so every stage is **768** wide (fighters stay between 280 and 1000). The limits are not
in the stage data: stage set-up (0x0881ECA4) gives a layer in camera mode 1 the constants 0x100 and
0x280, and every stage's fighters' layer is in mode 1. The other layers have no limits (-1): they
scroll at their own rate as the camera moves (parallax).

| Address | What |
|---|---|
| 0x092E88A0 | the fighters' layer (layers are 0xA0 bytes apart from 0x092E8800): +2 camera mode, +34 camera x (s16, screen left, also as 16.16 at +32), +68 left limit, +70 right limit |
| 0x092E1228 | s16: the stage being fought on |
| 0x08E759D1 | s8: the STAGE row's choice on the select screen (0 RANDOM, 1-38 as in the table) |
| 0x08A2B3E6 | u8[64]: choice - 1 -> stage number; RANDOM picks entry `rand & 63` (the 38 stages then a second run of the main ones, so the BATTLE stages and the second versions come up less often) |

| 0x089F3200 | char *[42]: stage number -> archive name (`st00.pac` ... `st29.pac`) |
| 0x08A0F718 | char *[42]: the name of each slot (RYU ... INGRID), same numbering; 0x08A120DC is the Japanese spelling (GOUKI, NASH, ...) |

### The four stage numbers you can't pick

Stage numbers run 0x00 - 0x29, which is 42, but the disc has only the 38 archives above. The other
four numbers are not missing stages: the archive table points each one at a stage that already
exists, and the name table gives the slot a name. None of them appears among the 64 entries of the
select screen's table (0x08A2B3E6), so FREE BATTLE never reaches them, RANDOM included.

| Stage | Slot name | Loads | Which is the stage of |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x12 | CHUN-LI | st04.pac | CHUNLI (0x04) |
| 0x13 | GEN | st11.pac | GEN (0x11) |
| 0x14 | SODOM | st06.pac | SODOM (0x06) |
| 0x1F | JUNI | st1e.pac | BISON2 (0x1E, whose slot is JULI) |

For 0x00 - 0x24 the stage number is the character number of the stage's owner (0x17 is E.RYU, so RYU
2 is Evil Ryu's stage; 0x24 is S.AKUMA, so AKUMA 2 is Shin Akuma's). 0x12 - 0x14 are second CHUN-LI,
GEN and SODOM slots, and JUNI shares JULI's stage. From 0x25 on the numbers stop matching:
the character slots 0x25 - 0x29 are BALROG, EAGLE, MAKI, YUN and INGRID, while the stages are BATTLE1
- 4 and KARIN 2.

## Widescreen

WIDE FORCED shows 480 pixels instead of 384, 48 more on each side: at the limits the view spans 208 -
1072 instead of 256 - 1024. Where a stage's art stops short of that, the edge is black (KNOWN_ISSUES
M7). The last two columns are that black strip at each edge with the camera at its limit, in pixels
(0 - 48): the widest (the layer whose art is shortest) and the narrowest (often 0 where the floor or a
foreground layer covers the edge).

- **No black edges:** GUILE and BATTLE1 - 4; FEILONG on the right.
- **Nearly none:** MIKA (10 px), ZANGIEF (14 px), BIRDIE on the right and KARIN 1 on the left (16 px).
- **About a full strip (up to 48 px):** ADON, SODOM, ROLENTO, CAMMY, HONDA, DEEJAY, and one side of
  several more.

**Decided: extend the art and keep the limits.** The limits also set where the walls are: fighters
stay 24 px inside the 4:3 view, between 280 and 1000. Pulling the limits in would make the stage
narrower and move the corners, which changes how fights play and breaks the WIDE FORCED parity rule.
Instead, every stage gets a **stage extension**, a 48 × 288 strip per side. The game will draw it
behind the stage's art, only in WIDE FORCED, so NORMAL and WIDE are untouched. Magenta placeholders
are committed in `assets/extensions/stages/<number>_<name>/`, ready to be painted. The size, placement, painting
notes and a per-stage table of what each strip shows are in
[STAGE_EXTENSIONS.md](STAGE_EXTENSIONS.md).

The table above counts the screen's rows 60 - 229 with the fighters on the ground. STAGE_EXTENSIONS.md
measures all 288 rows the strip covers, camera raised as well. So some stages that show 0 here
(GUILE, BATTLE1 - 4) still need a strip: below the floor's art, and at the top mid-jump.

More on what works and what doesn't in widescreen, and what a revision must keep: [WIDESCREEN.md](WIDESCREEN.md).
